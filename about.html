About: This project & The Author

About The Author

Lillian Dickes is a College student at Ensign and takes great pride in all of her work. Even this website, which she programmed herself, was a labor of learning and love. Lillian was born and raised in the United States and has been fascinated by Japan since she began learning to draw from "How to Draw Manga" books. This love has reached farther than their media and into their physical geography and culture as a whole. She's been taking her time with it, but is slowly learning Japanese and hopes to visit the Japanese countryside one day.

About Japan

If you've ever heard of Japan, a few things might come to mind: their flag, their cherry trees, Mount Fuji, and, if you know it well enough, Hot springs. Sitting right between not just two, but four tectonic plates (the Pacific, Philippine Sea, Eurasian, and North American plates), Japan is also prone to many horrific natural disasters such as Hurricanes, Tsunamis, and Earthquakes. This causes Japan's geology to change constantly! Now what does this have to do with the hot springs we're so interested in? Japan's location along the Ring of Fire makes it a prime breeding ground for hot springs! This is due to the proximity of the edges of these 4 Tectonic plates, which allows magma to heat the water beneath the country naturally! Now, if that's not cool, I'm not sure what is!

Each country and land is unique and beautiful. Our Earth is a marvel, and it is only by a master's hands that all this beauty could be achieved. Japan's placement might seem like a happenstance. Still, I can assure you it isn't: "The Lord made all that he did to prosper in his hand." (Genesis 39:3). Next time you see the sunshine carve space between the morning mountain, or that small creek in your neighborhood, remember it was all placed there for a reason.
